## What's new

**Public or private, for every course and week.** You can now keep a course, or a single week, off the marketplace.

- **New course** and **Add a week** have a **Visibility** setting. It starts at **Public**, so the course or week can go on the marketplace as before. Pick **Private** to keep it off.
- A new week starts with its course's setting. Every week in a private course is private too, whatever the week is set to.
- To change it later, use **Make course private…** in a course's ⋯ menu, or **Make week private…** in a week's ⋯ menu. **Make … public** changes it back.
- Private courses show a **Private** badge on the Courses page and the course page. Private weeks show one on the course page and the week page.

**Private means it's never published.** Publishing it by hand, **Publish course to marketplace…**, **Publish all my courses** and **Publish my weeks automatically** all skip private courses and weeks, and their **Publish** menu items are hidden.

**Making something private takes it down.** If a week is already on the marketplace when you make it (or its course) private, Studywise asks first, then takes it down. Nobody new can add it, but anyone who already added it keeps their copy. If the marketplace can't be reached, Studywise tells you which weeks are still up, and you can take them down from **Marketplace → Published by you**.

**Making something public again** publishes its written weeks automatically if **Publish my weeks automatically** is on. If it's off, publish them from the ⋯ menu when you're ready.

Every course and week you already have is public, and nothing is published that wasn't before. Whether something is private is saved on your computer and syncs to your phone. It isn't included when you share a week as a file.

## Download

If you have 1.8.3 or later on Windows, Studywise offers this update by itself: it downloads in the background and installs when you restart.

Otherwise, on Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
