## What's new

**Updates itself from now on.** Installed copies check for new releases soon after they open and every two hours.
- **Windows:** the update downloads in the background, and a banner offers **Restart to update**. Otherwise it installs the next time you quit.
- **Mac:** the banner says there's a new version, and **Download** opens it. Drag it to Applications to replace the old one.

This is the first version that can do this, so install this one by hand. Your courses, decks and notes stay.

**"Someone has already made this," by course and week.** When you add a week, Studywise now checks whether someone has already published that week of what looks like the same course, even before you add any slides. Week 9 of "Databases" finds Week 9 of "Database Systems (COMP23111)". Before you plan a week it still checks for a week made from the same slides. Either way, **Use this week** fills your week from theirs at no cost.

**Courses on the marketplace match under different names.** "Software Engineering 1 (COMP23311)" and "Software Engineering 1" are now one course, with everyone's weeks in it, under the name with the module code. Two different module codes are never merged.

**Publish your weeks automatically.** Turn on **Publish my weeks automatically** in Settings → Marketplace. Each week is published once its topics finish writing, and updated when you write more. It's off until you turn it on. Weeks you added from someone else are never published automatically.

**Your own weeks.** Weeks you published don't offer **Add to my courses** (you already have them), and don't count when Studywise checks whether someone else has already made a week.

**Phone sync keeps your latest change.** When your phone's clock was a little behind your computer's, a change made on the phone just after syncing (a topic you finished, a card you edited) could be lost at the next sync. A change made after syncing now always counts as the newer one. Update the phone app too, since it records the time of its own changes.

## Download

Mac: download `Studywise-Courses.dmg`, open it and drag Studywise Courses to Applications. Windows: download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. If you already have Studywise Courses, it updates in place and keeps your courses, decks and progress (a backup of your library is saved first).

You need Claude Code installed and logged in, or an Ollama server or an Ollama Cloud key:

```
npm install -g @anthropic-ai/claude-code
claude
```

The app isn't signed. On a Mac, right-click it and pick Open the first time. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
