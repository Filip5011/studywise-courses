## What's new

**Blank weeks you already have can be filled from the marketplace.** Until now, Studywise only checked whether someone had already made a week at the moment you added or planned it. Now any week with no topics yet keeps checking:
- On the course page, blank weeks that someone has already published are marked **Already made**.
- Open one, and a banner shows who made it, how many topics are written and which model wrote them, with **Use this week…**. Using it fills your week from theirs at no cost.
- A week's ⋯ menu has **Find on marketplace…** to check again whenever you like.

If you'd rather make a week yourself, pick **I'll make it myself** and its banner goes away.

## Download

If you have 1.8.3, Studywise offers this update by itself: on Windows it downloads in the background and installs when you restart, and on a Mac the banner's **Download** button opens it.

Otherwise: Mac, download `Studywise-Courses.dmg`, open it and drag Studywise Courses to Applications. Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On a Mac, right-click it and pick Open the first time. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
