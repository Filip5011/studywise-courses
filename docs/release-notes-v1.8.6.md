## What's new

**One row per week on the marketplace.** When several people have published the same week of a course, the course's marketplace page no longer lists every copy one after another.

- Each week number shows once. If there's more than one version of it, the week says how many (for example **Week 3 · 2 versions**) and has a dropdown to choose which one you see.
- Each version in the dropdown shows who made it, the model it was written with and how many people have downloaded it. Yours is marked **(you)**.
- The most downloaded version is shown first, then the most recently updated.
- Pick a version to see its topics, then **Add to my courses** as usual. If you take down the version you're looking at, the next one is shown.
- Weeks that only one person has published look the same as before.

## Download

If you have 1.8.3 or later on Windows, Studywise offers this update by itself: it downloads in the background and installs when you restart.

Otherwise, on Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
