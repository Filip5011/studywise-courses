## What's new

**Same weeks grouped properly on the marketplace.** When several people have published the same week of a course, it now shows once, with how many people made it.

- Each week is one row, for example **Week 3** with **4 people generated** next to it, plus the number of versions, total downloads and the models used.
- Click the week to open every version of it, most downloaded first. Each shows who made it and has its own **Add to my courses**. Yours is marked **(you)**.
- Weeks count as the same by the number in their name, so one person's "Week 3" and another's "W3: Testing" go together, even if one of them skipped a week in their own course. Before, they were matched by their place in each person's course, so they often didn't group at all.
- Weeks that only one person has published look the same as before.

**Updates show up sooner.** Installed copies now check for a new version every 30 minutes and when you come back to the window (it used to be every two hours), and **Settings → About** has **Check for updates** to check now.

- If an update fails to download, the banner says so and **Try again** downloads it again. Before, it showed the wrong message and its button didn't work.
- **Later** hides the banner until the update is ready to install, or until a newer version comes out, instead of for the rest of the session.
- Update problems are written to the log (`logs/main.log` in the data folder), so they can be tracked down.

## Download

If you have 1.8.3 or later on Windows, Studywise offers this update by itself: it downloads in the background and installs when you restart.

Otherwise, on Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
