## What's new

**Quick review of a week.** Once a week has written topics, its page has a **Quick review** button next to Write.

- It writes a quick revision version of each written topic: shorter sections that take less time, still covering nearly everything. You can add your own instructions first.
- It's saved alongside the notes you already have. Those stay as they are and stay shown, with your progress, and the recall cards are reused, so you don't get duplicates.
- **Show the week's notes as** switches the whole week between your notes and the quick review, and each topic has its own switch too. **Add a quick review** under a topic does just that one.
- Sharing or publishing the week includes both versions, and the marketplace shows which ones each topic has.

**Essay questions say how many marks they're worth.** "In your own words" questions, the ones Claude marks, now show what they're out of, for example **3 marks**: one for each key point in the mark scheme.

- Your answer is marked in marks, for example **2 / 3 marks**, with the points you got and the ones you missed.
- **Hint** (or H) is optional and gives hints one at a time: a nudge first, then, if you ask for more, each point the mark scheme gives a mark for.
- **I was right** gives full marks.

**Publish all my courses no longer uploads everything again.** On the Marketplace page (and **Publish course to marketplace…**), only weeks that are new, were taken down, or have changed since you published them are uploaded. The rest are skipped, and the dialog says how many. Studying a week doesn't count as changing it.

## Download

If you have 1.8.3 or later on Windows, Studywise offers this update by itself: it downloads in the background and installs when you restart.

Otherwise, on Windows, download `Studywise-Courses-Setup.exe` and run it. Your courses, decks and progress stay.

The app isn't signed. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
